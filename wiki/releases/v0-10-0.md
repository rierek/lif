## Headline

Deleting an item also deletes its `attachments/<id>/` directory. Before, the directory stayed behind without an owner. The delete confirmation names the attachment files.

## New commands and flags

No new commands or flags. `show` now lists the attachment files of an item.

## Fixes

- The `ready` filter takes its archive statuses from the config instead of a fixed list.
- The `just install` recipe is guarded and reports the build tree.

## Upgrade notes

Attachments are removed together with the item. Move files you want to keep out of the attachment directory before you delete the item.
