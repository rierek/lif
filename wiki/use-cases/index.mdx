**Find the page that looks like your work and start from its setup.** A use case shows one complete solution to a recognizable problem. It does not walk through every command: [Features](../features/) explain a capability and [Reference](../reference/) lists every command.

- [Personal tasks](personal-tasks/): a flat list with tags, preset `todo` or `simple`.
- [Home lab](homelab/): a lean setup with custom types.
- [Software project](software-projects/): milestones, epics and features beside the code, preset `classic`.
- [Release management](release-management/): releases that name the commit of every finished item, preset `complex`.
- [Writing or research project](writing-research/): custom types for work that is not software.

Preset names and type names are starting points, not a fixed model. `lif init --profile` writes a preset into your configuration, and you can change it later. See [Configuration](../reference/configuration/).

Experiments are not use cases. They live in the [Blog](../blog/) with the tag `experiment`.
